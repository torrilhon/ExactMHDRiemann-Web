Exact MHD Riemann Solver

Exact solutions of Riemann problems of one-dimensional ideal magnetohydrodynamics (γ-law gas), following M. Torrilhon, Exact Solver and Uniqueness Conditions for Riemann Problems of Ideal Magnetohydrodynamics, SAM Research Report 2002-06, ETH Zürich.
